# Birdsong recordings

Each file here is a clip of up to 12 seconds from the recording below. It was cut from the loudest
part, mixed to mono (the loon uses its left channel only, since its two channels partly cancel out),
evened out in loudness, faded in and out so it loops without a click, and encoded as 64 kbps MP3.
All were found on Wikimedia Commons, and each licence is CC0, public domain or CC BY.
